How to Recruit Agents

Recruiting sub-agents is a different audience than recruiting students — you're pitching to consultants and agencies, not families. Different channels, different message.

LinkedIn

Post the recruitment pitch as a normal post + directly message education consultants in your target countries. Best for credibility — agents check a company's LinkedIn before replying.

Facebook Groups

Search "[Country] education agents" / "study abroad consultants [Country]" groups and post there directly — this is where most independent agents actually hang out.

WhatsApp (direct)

Highest reply rate. Use the "Draft WhatsApp Message" button in each Agent's profile to send the pitch straight to their number, pre-filled and ready to send.
